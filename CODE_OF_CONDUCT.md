# Code Of Conduct

OZM Skills is a governance and documentation-heavy project. The community works best when discussion stays specific, evidence-aware, and scoped to the repository.

Expected behavior:

- be respectful and direct;
- focus criticism on claims, evidence, docs, tests, or governance rules;
- keep security-sensitive details out of public channels;
- separate proposals from accepted repository behavior;
- avoid presenting OZM as a production runtime, Agent OS, or commercial support product without maintainer-approved evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, spam, credential or private-data exposure, and repeated attempts to bypass repository security or contribution boundaries.

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions, pull requests, and community links to keep the project usable and safe.
