# Draft Depth Floor

Use this reference when a draft is reported as shallow, summary-only, too thin, or not useful for later execution.

Do not use word count as the depth metric.

## Section Depth Floor

Every core section should have:

- one main claim
- one to three evidence references
- one why-it-matters explanation
- one boundary, counterexample, failure mode, or explicit non-claim
- one effect on downstream execution, review, acceptance, or decision
- one unresolved question or clear reason no unresolved question remains

## Shallow Draft Signals

- conclusion without mechanism
- process without judgment
- owner-file restatement without synthesis
- benefits without failure modes
- recommendation without acceptance method
- headings without section-level claim/evidence rows
- "closed loop" language without issue-to-delta-to-verdict chain
- implementation advice without consumer action

When these signals appear, lower the ceiling to `shallow_summary_only`, `evidence_incomplete`, or `review_pending` until repaired.
