# Draft Quality Diffgate

Use this reference from `ozm-review-diffgate-acceptance` before calling a governed text artifact accepted, deepened, closed-loop, future-thread-safe, implementation-ready, or equivalent.

## Review Axes

- `coverage`: required questions, perspectives, sections, and downstream consumer needs.
- `evidence`: source refs, owner evidence, opened web/paper sources, or lowered non-claim wording for each core claim.
- `reasoning`: evidence connects to conclusions through a reasoning bridge.
- `depth`: mechanism, why-it-matters, boundary, counterexample, failure mode, or unresolved-question posture where relevant.
- `coherence`: section order advances the reader's decision rather than following source-file order by default.
- `actionability`: the intended reader can decide, execute, review, accept, continue, or debug.
- `non_claims`: assumptions, unknowns, stale sources, and deferred claims are explicit and ceiling-lowered.
- `issue_closure`: P0/P1 issues are verified closed with revision refs and reader/editor verdicts; P2 deferrals state claim effect.
- `review_synthesis`: findings were shape-validated, confidence-gated, deduped, contradiction-preserved, and traced to reviewer roles or source evidence.
- `flow_completeness`: for specs/plans/design docs, entry points, decision points, terminal states, unhappy paths, permission boundaries, and integration handoffs are covered or explicitly non-claimed.
- `feasibility_shadow_paths`: for implementation-bearing text, happy, nil/missing, empty, and upstream-error paths are covered where they affect reader action.
- `evaluation_bias`: reader/editor verdicts note obvious length, style, authority, position/order, and self-review bias risks when an LLM judge or same-thread reviewer is used.

## Verdicts

- `accepted_text`
- `accepted_with_deferred_P2`
- `draft_candidate`
- `evidence_incomplete`
- `shallow_summary_only`
- `reader_review_required`

A generated file, long prose section, or successful formatting pass is not text acceptance. If the issue-to-delta-to-verdict chain is missing, keep the claim below accepted wording.

## Specialist Boundary

Use preserved text specialists for domain detail only after OZM freezes the artifact authority and closeout policy. Their PASS/clean result is not accepted text by itself; it becomes one reviewer verdict row subject to claim ceiling and source reread.
