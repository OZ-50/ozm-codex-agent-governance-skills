# Thesis-Driven Drafting

Use this reference when a governed text artifact risks becoming a source summary instead of a decision-support document.

Each core section needs:

- `thesis`: the claim or judgment the section is responsible for.
- `evidence_rows`: source-backed facts or observations.
- `reasoning_bridge`: why the evidence supports the thesis.
- `counter_evidence_or_boundary`: the strongest limitation, exception, or failure mode.
- `downstream_action`: what a human or future agent can do from the section.
- `claim_ceiling`: accepted, review pending, draft candidate, or non-claim.

Do not expand prose before the thesis row has evidence and a boundary. Long prose without these rows remains `shallow_summary_only`.
